Maypa et al. (2025) — Long-term reef monitoring: methods and baseline data

Published in Marine Ecology Progress Series | January 2025

Comprehensive assessment of coral and fish communities across 12 monitoring sites with standardized visual census techniques. This paper establishes baseline data for tracking long-term ecosystem changes and provides methodological protocols for reef monitoring.

Read full article →

Fukuda et al. (2024) — Restoration outcomes and statistical analysis

Published in Coral Reefs Journal | July 2024

Analysis of coral transplant survival rates and growth trajectories from multi-year restoration trials. Results demonstrate 70-80% survival for fragments of resilient coral species and identify species-specific management strategies for optimization.

Divinagracia et al. (2023) — Community-based restoration protocols

Published in Environmental Management | December 2023

Framework for community engagement in coral restoration activities. This study documents participatory approaches that strengthen local stewardship and build capacity while contributing to reef recovery goals.
