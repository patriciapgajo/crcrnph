About

Our Purpose

Vision

Thriving coral reef ecosystems that support biodiversity, human livelihoods, and climate resilience across generations.

Mission

To monitor, restore, and advocate for sustainable coral reef ecosystems through science, community collaboration, and open knowledge sharing.

The Network

A visual database of coral reef conservation and restoration efforts from a collaborative community of researchers, practitioners, and stewards with a unifying committment to the protection and awareness of coral reefs.

The Team

Aileen P. Maypa, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator

Dr. Aileen P. Maypa is an established marine scientist with a PhD in Zoology (Marine Biology). She has published multiple research articles on coral reef ecology, reef fish biology, marine protected area effectiveness, and coral reef recovery, and has received international recognition for her work in marine conservation. Her current research focuses on coral reef restoration, sustainable fisheries, and resilient coastal ecosystems.
